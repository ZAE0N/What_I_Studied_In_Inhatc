# A Design and Implementation of Online Exhibition Application for Disabled Artists

## Author's problem identification and argument

The authors identify a significant problem faced by disabled artists: the lack of exhibition spaces and opportunities to share their work with the public. Despite growing interest in art created by disabled individuals, there are limited venues for showcasing their work, which restricts their ability to reach wider audiences and gain recognition. The authors argue that an online exhibition platform could alleviate these issues by providing a virtual space for disabled artists to display their work. They propose an Android-based application that incorporates features such as Speech-to-Text (STT) and Text-to-Speech (TTS) to enhance accessibility for visually and hearing-impaired users. The application also includes a disability certification process to ensure that only authenticated disabled artists can exhibit their work. By utilizing technologies like MySQL for database management and Firebase Storage for handling large data sizes, the application aims to overcome the limitations of physical exhibition spaces and facilitate better communication between disabled artists and the general public. The authors acknowledge that while the application addresses some accessibility issues, it is limited in diversity and could be improved by considering a wider range of disabilities and artistic fields in future research.

## Introduction Author

Seung Gyeom Kim, Ha Ram Kang, Tae Hun Kim, Jun Hyeok Lee, and Won Joo Lee are affiliated with Hanyang University and Inha Technical College in Korea. They have backgrounds in computer science and engineering, with research interests spanning computer vision, human-computer interaction, mobile programming, web programming, and machine learning. Their collective expertise has contributed to the design and implementation of an online exhibition application aimed at supporting disabled artists by providing a platform that addresses the challenges of limited exhibition opportunities and enhances accessibility through innovative technological solutions.
